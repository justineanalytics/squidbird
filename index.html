Public art · Chicago · 2026In progress

Is the American Dream Dead?

In 1980, Studs Terkel interviewed 100 Americans about the American Dream. So I brought him back, sort of. A resurrected skeleton Terkel sits at a booth with Tuxedo, Vaiku AI's offline conversational speaker, and asks Chicagoans the same questions before the 2026 midterms. The goal is 100 new interviews.

Then I compare themes and keywords from Terkel's interviews with today's answers, by generation, and write it up as a comedic-journalism piece. Consent comes first. Only generation and themes get published unless someone opts in.

About

Hi, I'm Justine. Part squid, part bird.

I started as a research assistant at the University of Illinois, moved into people analytics, and then spent about 15 years in advertising and digital media. I ran social analytics teams, built AI applications, and mined big social data for Fortune 500 brands.

Somewhere along the way I noticed that everything I make, at work or not, comes back to the same three things: art, data, and fairness. So Squidbird is now where they all live together. (The art itself lives over at my art page.)

I care a lot about AI ethics. I'd rather show a small team how to do more with less than sell anyone a shiny thing they don't need.
