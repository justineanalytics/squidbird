About

Hi, I'm Justine.

I started as a research assistant at the University of Illinois and moved into people analytics. Then I spent about 15 years in advertising and digital media, where I led social analytics and reporting teams, mined big social data for Fortune 500 brands, and was AI lead building AI applications. At VML, I led the shift from manual to automated social media reporting and worked on the agency's award-winning creative data team led by Jason Carmel.

Squidbird is my independent practice. I help startups and mission-driven teams plan and measure their social, get real answers out of Google Analytics and Ads, and automate the repetitive work, without needing a big agency budget. Today I also lead data operations at a Chicago workforce nonprofit, where many of the automations above were built.

I care a lot about data ethics. I'd rather show a small team how to do more with less than sell anyone a shiny thing they don't need.
